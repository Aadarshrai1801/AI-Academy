## **FAQ and enquiry copy** 

#### **Frequently asked questions** 

**Who are the corporate programs for?** Employees, executives, managers and senior leaders. Content depends on the participants’ roles and learning goals. 

**Do I need a technical background?** Introductory AI and digital programs can suit non-technical participants. Development and technical project tracks may require programming or data skills. The program outline explains the prerequisites. 

**Can training be tailored to our company?** A tailored scope can be discussed around your audience, priority tasks and preferred delivery format. 

**Are the internships employer placements?** The core student pathways involve supervised Academy project work. Employer-hosted opportunities, where available, are identified separately with a confirmed host and terms. 

**Will I receive a certificate or academic credit?** Completion requirements and any Academy certificate are specified in the program outline. Academic credit depends on your institute’s agreement. 

**Is a stipend available?** Any stipend depends on the specific arrangement and will be confirmed in writing. Do not assume that a stipend is included. 

**Does the program guarantee employment?** No. Project work, feedback and interview preparation support career readiness, but employment decisions remain with employers. 

**How much does a program cost?** Request the current outline or a corporate proposal for fees, inclusions and payment terms. Costs may vary by duration, delivery format and scope. 

**When does the next intake start?** Register your interest to enquire about available tracks and upcoming dates.
